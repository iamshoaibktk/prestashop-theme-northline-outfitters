Repairs for life

Every Northline jacket comes with free repairs, for as long as you own it. We would rather fix a zip than sell you a new coat.

  1. Send us a photoShow us the damage from your account or by email.
  2. We send a free labelPack the jacket and drop it at any parcel point.
  3. Back in two weeksRepaired in our Stockholm workshop and posted home.

How do the free repairs work?expand_more
Send us a photo of the damage. We email a prepaid label, repair the jacket and send it back within two weeks.
How do I choose my size?expand_more
Our fits are true to size. Take one size up if you wear a thick sweater underneath.
Is waxed cotton waterproof?expand_more
It sheds rain and wind. Re-wax it once a year to keep it that way.
